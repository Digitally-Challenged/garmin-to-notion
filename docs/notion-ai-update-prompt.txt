DATABASE: "Workouts"

Add these 4 new views (keep all existing views):

- "Intensity Split" — CHART view.
  Chart type: Donut.
  Filter: Date is on or after 2025-01-01.
  Measure: Count of pages (use "Count", not a specific property).
  Slice by: Intensity.
  Style: medium height, show data labels, show legend.

- "Weekly Volume" — CHART view.
  Chart type: Vertical bar.
  Filter: Date is on or after 2025-06-01.
  X-axis: Date (group by Week).
  Y-axis: Count of pages.
  Sort: ascending.
  Style: medium height, show data labels, show grid lines.

- "Calories Burned" — CHART view.
  Chart type: Vertical bar.
  Filter: 2 conditions, AND:
    Date is on or after 2025-06-01
    Calories is not empty
  X-axis: Date (group by Month).
  Y-axis: Calories (Number property, SUM).
  Sort: ascending.
  Color/Group: Modality.
  Style: medium height, stacked bars, show data labels.

- "This Week" — Table view.
  Filter: Date is within "This week".
  Sort: Date descending.
  Show: Workout, Date, Modality, Intensity, Duration, Calories, Avg HR.
  Hide: Distance (km), Avg Pace, Source, Strava ID.
